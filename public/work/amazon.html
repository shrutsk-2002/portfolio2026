The team

Making every title feel local.

Prime Video streams in 240+ countries and territories across 27 languages, and every title has to be localized before it gets there. That used to mean manual hand-offs between localization service providers, production, marketing and every team in between. We supported the whole chain end to end, and set out to automate it, track it and make it flow.

My role

Two areas, one designer.

Localization has many moving parts. I owned two of them, content localization and marketing localization, with a product for each: Localization Central and Pixel. As the sole designer on both, I owned the whole process: user research, IA, product design, user testing and visual QA. I partnered with business, product, engineering and QA to keep quality high from first sketch to ship.
